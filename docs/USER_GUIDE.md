# How to use ReWear

ReWear helps students wear more of the clothes they already own so they buy less.

## Build and use your closet

1. In Closet, tap **Add garment** or **Add your first garment**. Take a photo or choose one from your gallery.
2. Choose **Fit whole garment**, **Manual crop**, or **AI smart crop**. Tap **Use front photo**. Choose **Edit photo** to rotate it or remove the background.
3. If you want help, tap **Suggest name and details from photo**. Check the name, category and color before you save.
4. Add at least one top, one garment in Bottoms, and one pair of shoes.
5. In Style Me, choose **University** or **Interview** and tap **Suggest an outfit**. Tap **Try again** for another look.
6. Tap **Save outfit**.
7. In Saved, tap **I wore this** after you wear the outfit.
8. Check your 30-day **ReWear Score** in Closet. The **Closet gap** card in Style Me shows which one extra piece would unlock the most outfits.

## Privacy

Your closet and photos are stored on this device in this browser. Outfit requests send garment names, categories and colors, along with internal item IDs, the occasion and recent outfit IDs. When you tap Suggest name and details from photo, Gemini receives only the cropped photo you select. AI smart crop sends a resized view of the photo so Gemini can locate the garment.

## Limits and tips

AI can misread dark, patterned or low-contrast garments. Use good light and a plain background, and review every suggestion. Your closet works offline while the app is open. AI suggestions need internet, and an offline reload may fail. Clearing this site’s browser data deletes your closet.

## Install on your phone

On Android, open ReWear in Chrome. Open the browser menu and choose Add to Home screen or Install app. On iPhone, open ReWear in Safari, tap Share, and choose Add to Home Screen.

## Troubleshooting

- **Camera permission.** Allow camera access if your browser asks. If you previously blocked it, check this site’s camera permission or choose a photo from your gallery.
- **No internet.** Keep the app open to use the local closet. Reconnect to request AI smart crop, photo details, or outfit suggestions.
- **Gemini unavailable.** If you run ReWear yourself, check the server’s `GEMINI_API_KEY` and restart it. Enter garment details manually and use fit or manual crop in the meantime.

## Demo script

Aim for two to three minutes. Start with enough saved garments to cover Tops, Bottoms and Shoes.

1. Explain the problem. Students buy more when they cannot see what they can already wear.
2. Add a garment from a photo. Show the crop, request AI details, review the name, category and color, and save.
3. Get an outfit for University. Switch to Interview, then show the new outfit and reason.
4. Save the outfit. In Saved, tap **I wore this**. Return to Closet to show the wear counts and ReWear Score.
5. Open Style Me and explain the Closet gap calculation. Turn on airplane mode with the page open and show the offline message.
